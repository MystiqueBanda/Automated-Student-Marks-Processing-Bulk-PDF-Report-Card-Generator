[0:45 - 1:30] MANUAL PROCESS VS. PROPOSED AUTOMATION PIPELINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Visual: Switch screen to Slide 5 comparison table, then zoom into Workflow/Workflow_Architecture.png]

ADITYA (0:45 - 1:08):
"Let's look at our system architecture on screen. Rather than relying on manual intervention, our solution implements a 4-stage pipeline. 
Stage 1 programmatically ingests raw student Excel spreadsheets using Pandas and OpenPyXL. Immediately at Stage 2, our outer Exception Scenario 1 guard validates file existence and verifies that all mandatory headers—Student ID, Name, Math, Science, English, and Attendance—are strictly present."

PUSHKAL (1:08 - 1:30):
"If the file passes, Stage 3 initiates our row-level validation loop, guarded by Exception Scenario 2. Here, every score is type-checked and validated against academic boundaries between 0 and 100. If any dirty record is encountered, the system never crashes—it isolates the row, logs the error into `error_log.txt`, and seamlessly processes the remaining cohort. 
Finally, Stage 4 calculates total marks, percentage, and assigned grades, synthesizing vectorized PDF report cards via ReportLab and a styled master Excel sheet."
